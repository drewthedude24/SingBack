# Demo song sources

These five vocal tracks were downloaded from Pixabay and reduced to ten-second
demo excerpts. Each excerpt was separated offline with Demucs (`htdemucs`,
two-stem vocal mode) into a reference vocal and an instrumental. The original
full-length downloads are intentionally not stored in this repository.

All five source pages identify their audio as free for use under the
[Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/).
Attribution is not required by the license summary, but source and creator
records are kept here for provenance. Review the current license and source
page before any public release outside the hackathon demo.
